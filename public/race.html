House rules. One polar for the whole fleet, scaled to of polar targets. The engine sails your waypoints through real surface wind , stepping — it never rewinds, so what's sailed is sailed. Every tack or gybe costs , so a wiggly routing is a slow routing, and water under drags you to half speed — mind the shoals. Miss a mark and the committee quietly bends your route back through it: never disqualified, never shortened. Route with the forecasts you'd have on board; the ocean grades your homework in real time.
