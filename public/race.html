Why the gap?

The time between a real boat and a virtual boat is split two ways. Boat speed: the real boat's own track re-sailed at polar speed through the same wind, so how far above or below the polar it actually sails. Navigation: everything else, meaning where each boat went, and when. Click a leaderboard row to pick a boat.

Forecasts (GRIB)

The wind the fleet is sailing in, saved every 6 hours from the same model that drives the boats (Open-Meteo, 10 m wind, GRIB-1). Load a file into your routing software. Older files show what was known at the time.

House rules. One polar for the whole fleet, scaled to of the polar. Your boat sails your waypoints through real surface wind , advancing . What's sailed is locked. Every tack or gybe costs , and water under drops you to half speed. Miss a mark and your route is bent back through it: no disqualification, no shortened course.

Not for navigation. Virtual Navigator is a simulation. Nothing here may be used to navigate a vessel.

Your data. An account is a username, a display name and a password hash, plus an email address if you choose to add one, used only to send a password-reset link. Sailed tracks and submission times are public; future waypoints are not. The session cookie lasts 90 days.
