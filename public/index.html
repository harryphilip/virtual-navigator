fantasy offshore racing — route it like you're on board

How it works

Pick a race, register a virtual boat, and submit a routing from your own navigation software — like fantasy baseball, but your lineup is a set of waypoints. Every virtual boat sails the same polar through the same real weather; the leaderboard ranks you against the other armchair navigators and the real boats on the tracker.

  1. Download the race polar and load it into Expedition, Adrena, qtVlm, TimeZero, LuckGrib, PredictWind — anything that can export a route as GPX or CSV.
  2. Run your own weather routing with the forecasts you'd have on board, then upload the route here as your routing.
  3. Update as the race unfolds — but only the part of the course you haven't sailed yet. The engine advances your boat in (near) real time through actual wind, so the past is locked and routings can never go back in time. No hindsight, no replaying yesterday's shift.
  4. Your future routing stays private until sailed; your wake is public, just like a real race tracker.

⚡ Auto-create from the Notice of Race / Sailing Instructions

Drop in the official race documents (PDF or text) and a virtual race is created automatically — name, start time, and course marks are read from the documents, which are attached to the race page for competitors. Marks are only taken from positions stated in the documents, never guessed. AI document reading needs Anthropic credentials on the server; otherwise a pattern parser handles documents with coordinate tables.

    Connecting your navigation software
    
    
    Getting the polar in
    
    
    Each race page offers the polar as a .pol text file
    (TWA rows × TWS columns) — the format read by Expedition, qtVlm, OpenCPN
    weather_routing, Adrena and most others. Apply the race performance factor
    if you want your routing to match the engine exactly.
    
    
    Getting your routing out
    
    
    Export the optimal route as GPX (universally supported) or CSV with
    lat/lon columns, and upload it on the race page. Only the waypoints matter —
    the engine sails them in order at polar speed through real wind.
    
    
    Getting your boat back in
    
    
    Download your sailed track as GPX from the race page and overlay it in your
    software against the GRIBs to see where your routing won or lost.
    
    
    Real fleet
    
    
    Race admins can import tracker positions for the real boats (GPX track or
    CSV time,lat,lon — e.g. exported from YB/Yellowbrick race viewers)
    and tag each with a class label, so the leaderboard shows how you stack up
    against the boats actually sailing your polar.
