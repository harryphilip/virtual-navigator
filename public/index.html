How it works

Route the real race. Sail the real weather.

Pick an offshore race that is actually on and submit a route. Your boat sails it through today's wind and current, on the same leaderboard as the real fleet. One polar for everyone: the routing wins, not the boat. Your route locks in as it is sailed, so results reflect what you knew when you chose. Afterwards the gap to any real boat is split into boat speed and navigation, so you know whether they were faster or smarter.

First race? Start here

You don't need routing software or offshore experience. The boat is virtual; the weather is real.

  1. Create an account below, open an upcoming or live race, and enter a boat on its page.
  2. Draw a route. Click Draw on chart, click a line of waypoints from start to finish, and submit. A straight line is a fine first route. The leaderboard will show you why the leaders didn't sail one.
  3. Check the weather once a day. Download the race's on-board forecast (a GRIB file with the same wind your boat sails in) and adjust the part of your route you haven't sailed yet.
  4. Try routing software. qtVlm is free. Import the race's course route and polar, load the GRIB, compute a route, export it as GPX and upload it here.
  5. Compare afterwards. Download your sailed track as GPX and overlay it on the real fleet's to see where you gained and lost.

The full rules, the weather model and the file formats are on How it works.

Account

Forgot password?

The link works once, for one hour, and only if the account has an email on file. Add one on your profile page.

3 to 30 characters for the username; letters, digits, dash or underscore. Passwords are at least 6 characters. Without an email on file there is no way to reset a forgotten password, so write it down.

Create a race from the Notice of Race / Sailing Instructions

Upload the official race documents (PDF or text). The name, start time and course marks are read from them, and the documents are attached to the race page. Marks are only taken from positions stated in the documents, never guessed. Reading prose needs Anthropic credentials on the server; without them, a pattern parser reads coordinate tables.

    Link the real fleet (YB Tracking)

    If the real race is on yb.tl, enter its slug (the part after yb.tl/) to import the fleet. The server then polls positions every 10 minutes. The optional filter keeps only boats whose model matches, e.g. J/122.
