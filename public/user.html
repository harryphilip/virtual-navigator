Your data. An account is a username, a display name and a password hash. No email is kept, so there is no password reset. Sailed tracks and submission times are public; future waypoints are not. The session cookie lasts 90 days. Pages load fonts from Google and map tiles from OpenStreetMap and NOAA, which see your IP address.
