How it works

The link in the email works once and for one hour. Every other session on this account is signed out when the password changes.

Not for navigation. Virtual Navigator is a simulation. Nothing here may be used to navigate a vessel.

Wind and current: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Charts: NOAA ENC, OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenSeaMap. Bathymetry: NOAA NCEI. Polars: meltemus.com. Real-boat positions: YB Tracking and aisstream.io, shown for comparison only. Not affiliated with any race organiser.

Your data. An account is a username, a display name and a password hash, plus an email address if you choose to add one, used only to send a password-reset link. Sailed tracks and submission times are public; future waypoints are not. The session cookie lasts 90 days. Pages load fonts from Google and map tiles from OpenStreetMap and NOAA, which see your IP address.
